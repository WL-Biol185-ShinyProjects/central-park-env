# central-park-env
We selected this squirrel data set from theNYC open source data because we wanted to do something that intersected
with all of our academic interests. Being a group with broad interests, we started to look at interesting datasets
to help us decide on a topic. We came across this  squirrel dataset that fascinated us because of the multitude of
observations that observers had made. From this data, we realized we could do quite a lot with the squirrels and 
each of us found different parts about the dataset interesting. The dataset had lots of opportunity to evaluate 
what recorded factors were influencing squirrel behavior as well as opportunities to visualize the diversity of 
squirrel populations in central park. Squirrels are also an interesting animal to study because they are important
for ecosystem health but can also be controversial based on public opinions. We hope you learn more about squirrels
and find them as fascinating as we do!
